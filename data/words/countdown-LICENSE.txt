The Countdown word list (countdown.txt) is drawn from SCOWL (https://wordlist.aspell.net), size 85
(huge+), every English spelling (US, GB with -ise and -ize, Canadian, Australian), variants up to
"archaic", so it takes the rare and old words a big dictionary has; lowercase a-z words of 2 to 9
letters. countdown-extra.txt adds real words it still lacks (slang, newer words). Its notice follows.

  Spelling: US GB CA AU
  Variant Level: 7 (archaic)
